# Biotechnology Notes

## Bioluminescence
Bioluminescence is the production of light by living organisms through a biochemical reaction.

## Mechanism
The enzyme luciferase catalyses the oxidation of luciferin, resulting in light emission.

## Examples
- Fireflies
- Jellyfish
- Dinoflagellates

## Applications
- Biomedical research
- Biosensors
- Gene expression studies
- Environmental monitoring
